Privacy Policy for Dearly

Last updated: October 2026

Dearly is built by Bondly. This policy explains how your data is handled. Your privacy is our priority.

1. Data Storage

All data you enter into Dearly (names, birthdays, sizes, wishlists, notes) is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. We do not have servers, and we do not transmit your personal data to the internet.

2. Data Collection & Sharing

We do not collect, track, or share any of your personal data. We do not use third-party analytics, and we do not show ads.

3. Notifications

Dearly asks for permission to send you local notifications. These are scheduled locally on your device to remind you of upcoming birthdays. No notification data is sent to our servers.

4. Deletion

You can permanently delete all your data at any time by going to Settings > Clear All Contacts, or by uninstalling the app.

5. Contact

If you have any questions, contact us at support@officialbondly.com.
